Our quality policy

What sets us apart

Puravankara is recognised for luxurious, state-of-the-art projects that incorporate innovative technology to redefine future homes — with a strong presence in commercial office spaces as well.

Quality Management System

A repository of processes and systems which define all activities at Puravankara, ensuring delivery of a quality product at optimum cost and minimum impact to the environment and society at large.

Project Management

A monthly review of ongoing projects where progress reports aligned with international best practices cover progress, cost, inventory, and risks — enabling quick decisions.

Environment, Health & Safety

We stand fully committed to Environment, Health and Safety, setting objectives and programmes and acting frequently to achieve continual improvement.

Sustainability

Sustainable Luxury, Built for the Future

Sustainability isn’t just a commitment, it’s a way of life. We blend eco-conscious design with luxurious living — energy-efficient materials, advanced water conservation systems, and effective waste management.

Our landscapes feature native plants, rainwater harvesting, and expansive green spaces, fostering a deep connection with nature. By prioritising sustainable innovation, Puravankara is shaping a greener tomorrow, one home at a time.
